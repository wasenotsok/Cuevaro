# Product Vision — Cuevaro

## Vision

Build the most trusted and effortless administrative memory for ordinary life.

Cuevaro should become the place a user can send the boring important thing without first deciding where it belongs, which fields to type, what calendar to use, or what future consequence they may be forgetting.

## North-star experience

A user should be able to:

1. capture or forward something in seconds;
2. see Cuevaro understand it;
3. correct only what is uncertain;
4. put it away;
5. receive a useful cue later;
6. complete the action with the original evidence and context already assembled.

If the product requires users to become diligent data-entry clerks, it has failed.

## Product principles

### P1 — Capture beats setup
Value should appear before the user configures a taxonomy.

### P2 — The evidence is sacred
The original receipt/document/photo is preserved and linked to every important derived fact.

### P3 — Facts have provenance
Every significant value has a source class:
- user confirmed;
- directly extracted;
- externally researched;
- computed;
- imported;
- inferred.

### P4 — Confidence changes the interface
High-confidence, low-risk facts may be accepted with lightweight review. Low-confidence or high-consequence facts require explicit confirmation.

### P5 — One capture can have many consequences
A purchase can create an asset, proof, warranty, return window, maintenance plan, and claim context. The user should not manually create six objects.

### P6 — Quiet until useful
Cuevaro should not manufacture engagement. No streaks, guilt loops, or notification spam. A quiet day is a product success.

### P7 — Action, not notification
A cue should answer:
- What happened / is approaching?
- Why does Cuevaro believe that?
- What do I need?
- What can I do now?
- What evidence is already available?

### P8 — User authority
AI proposes. The user can confirm, edit, dismiss, override, archive, export, or delete.

### P9 — No lock-in as a moat
Trust is stronger when the user can export records and originals in understandable formats.

### P10 — Sensitive by default
Assume captured data may contain personally identifying, financial, health, child, or legal information even if the current feature did not ask for it.

### P11 — Simple surface, serious internals
Users should see a calm interface. Complexity belongs behind the glass.

### P12 — Region-aware truth
Return policies, registration rules, renewal lead times, and similar information differ by merchant and jurisdiction. Cuevaro must attach source, jurisdiction, and checked-at time rather than pretending a universal rule.

## Long-term product shape

Cuevaro can grow through domains that share the same lifecycle engine:

### Cuevaro Purchases
Receipts, returns, warranties, proof, service, resale.

### Cuevaro Home
Appliances, maintenance, insurance evidence, property records.

### Cuevaro Auto
Vehicle purchase/service records, registration, insurance, maintenance.

### Cuevaro Family
Shared records, school admin, IDs, travel/admin information.

### Cuevaro Papers
Licenses, memberships, contracts, policies, certificates.

### Cuevaro Business — later
Licenses, employee certificates, equipment, insurance, vendor renewals, contract notice windows.

These should be **views over one canonical engine**, not six disconnected trackers.

## Commercial edge

The category already contains:
- document vaults;
- home inventory tools;
- warranty trackers;
- reminder apps;
- household organizers;
- AI chat-based life-admin tools.

Cuevaro should outperform not by having the longest feature list, but by making the whole lifecycle feel automatic and trustworthy.

### Desired defensible advantages

1. **Capture routing:** drop almost anything in without choosing a module first.
2. **Entity resolution:** recognize that today's service invoice relates to the refrigerator captured last year.
3. **Consequence graph:** derive future obligations from a record and its relationships.
4. **Evidence graph:** facts and actions remain traceable to originals.
5. **Confidence UX:** uncertainty is handled visibly instead of hidden.
6. **Action packs:** Cuevaro can assemble claim/return/renewal proof instead of just sending an alarm.
7. **Completion-aware lifecycle:** a completed action updates the future schedule.
8. **Calm attention:** priority by consequence and urgency rather than raw date sorting.
9. **Privacy and portability:** user control becomes part of the product promise.
10. **Provider abstraction:** AI/search/notification vendors can change without redefining the product.

## Anti-vision

Do not become:

- "Notion for your life";
- "another calendar";
- a dashboard with 40 widgets;
- an AI chat window with a filing cabinet behind it;
- a document graveyard;
- a subscription whose only ongoing value is access to files the user uploaded;
- a recommendation engine that sells or exploits personal life data;
- a product that scares users into uploading every sensitive document on day one.

## Adoption strategy

Trust and habit should expand gradually.

### Phase A: low-sensitivity utility
Purchases, receipts, warranties, return windows.

### Phase B: household utility
Appliances, maintenance, home inventory, vehicles.

### Phase C: shared life administration
Family access, school/admin records, memberships, policies.

### Phase D: higher-sensitivity records
IDs, insurance, selected medical/admin records only after security maturity, privacy controls, and user trust justify expansion.

## Experience benchmark

Cuevaro should aspire to this sentence:

> "I don't organize my life in Cuevaro. I send Cuevaro things, and it organizes the parts that will matter later."

That is the standard against which feature proposals should be judged.

## Mobile product doctrine

Cuevaro should feel native to the moment an administrative burden appears: at a checkout counter, beside an appliance label, while opening mail, or when a notice arrives on a phone. Mobile capture and fast confirmation are therefore the center of the experience. Desktop expands the workspace but does not define the product.

The product should remove clerical work, not relocate it onto a smaller screen.
