## Community problem signals

Reddit/community research reviewed recurring complaints around:
- lost receipts and expired warranty/free-service windows;
- forgetting to create reminders in the first place;
- family/school information scattered across email, PDFs, portals, screenshots, and memory;
- expiry tracking for passports, licences, registrations, and certifications;
- costly missed renewal/cancellation windows;
- manual home inventory setup;
- applications that feel like spreadsheets/forms with a subscription;
- desire for low-friction capture instead of database maintenance.

Community evidence is directional, not proof of willingness to pay. Product validation requires user interviews, alpha behavior, retention, and pricing tests.

## Research interpretation rules

- Competitor marketing claims are not treated as audited technical facts.
- Reddit anecdotes are problem signals, not market-size estimates.
- Search snippets are not authoritative sources for consequential user policy dates.
- Cuevaro runtime policy intelligence must prefer merchant/manufacturer/government/contract evidence and store source + checked-at timestamp.
- Commercial decisions should be updated as current competitor capabilities/pricing change.

## Current market conclusion

The category is validated but fragmented and competitive.

Cuevaro's defensible opportunity is not "being the only app that stores these things." It is delivering a substantially better **mobile evidence-to-lifecycle workflow** with trustworthy provenance, low setup burden, quality-gated capture, useful attention, and action preparation.
