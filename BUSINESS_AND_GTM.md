# Cuevaro — Business Model and Go-to-Market

Status: planning baseline  
Commercial intent: aspiring consumer software product with a later family and business path  
Owner rule: simplicity, convenience, trust, and real usefulness must beat feature count.

## 1. Commercial thesis

Cuevaro should be bought because it removes recurring administrative work rather than merely storing information.

The core paid value is:

**Capture once → Cuevaro understands the evidence → connects it to the right thing → watches future consequences → reminds at useful times → prepares the next action.**

A receipt app is easy to replace. A trusted lifecycle memory that already knows a household's purchases, documents, warranties, service history, evidence, and future obligations becomes progressively more useful.

## 2. Initial beachhead

Launch around purchases and household items:

- receipts;
- warranty evidence;
- return windows;
- model/serial information;
- service/maintenance history;
- product documents;
- lifecycle reminders;
- claim/return preparation.

Why this beachhead:
- easy to explain in one demo;
- lower trust barrier than asking for passports or health records on day one;
- immediate monetary value when a return/warranty deadline matters;
- camera-first and therefore naturally mobile;
- creates reusable entities for future home, vehicle, insurance, and family modules.

## 3. Customer promise

Cuevaro is not "another organizer."

Positioning:
> **Give Cuevaro something once. It remembers what matters and brings it back when you need to act.**

The promise must remain evidence-backed. Cuevaro should say **unknown** rather than invent a consequential deadline.

## 4. Reasons to choose Cuevaro

### 4.1 Less setup
Forms are fallback. The normal path is camera, share sheet, PDF, screenshot, forwarded evidence later, or voice context.

### 4.2 Lifecycle, not filing
Cuevaro links evidence to a real-world thing and follows what happens next: return, warranty, maintenance, renewal, expiry, claim, replacement.

### 4.3 Evidence-backed dates
Consequential dates show source/provenance and confidence rather than appearing as unexplained AI guesses.

### 4.4 Calm attention
Cuevaro should not generate dashboard guilt. If nothing matters today, the correct screen is **Nothing needs you today.**

### 4.5 Action preparation
A reminder should be able to prepare the receipt, serial number, policy source, checklist, and draft message needed for the next step.

### 4.6 Mobile-native
The product is designed around the phone camera and real-world moments rather than shrinking a desktop filing cabinet onto a phone.

## 5. Competitive reality

The category is validated and crowded in pieces:

- Itemtopia covers home inventory, receipts, warranties, service records, sharing, and AI.
- HomeZada covers home inventory, maintenance, projects, and home management.
- Stowly covers household inventory, warranties, receipts, and return deadlines.
- Life Admin covers household renewals, expiries, reminders, and documents.
- FileCue converts scanned documents into records, due dates, and reminders.
- other apps target individual return windows, deadline extraction, warranties, or subscription tracking.

Cuevaro should not compete by offering the longest checklist. It must win on **capture-to-action convenience, trustworthy provenance, lifecycle linking, and calm mobile UX**.

## 6. Monetization principles

1. Do not charge merely for the privilege of storing basic reminders.
2. Paid value should correspond to real recurring cost/value: AI extraction, policy intelligence, cross-device secure sync, household collaboration, active lifecycle watching, advanced action packs, and higher limits.
3. Users retain access to their confirmed records and export rights if a paid plan lapses; automation limits can change, but the product must not hold personal evidence hostage.
4. Never sell personal document content or ad-target from it.
5. Pricing must be understandable without a spreadsheet.

## 7. Pricing hypotheses for validation

These are experiments, not launch commitments.

### Free
Purpose: prove value.
- limited monthly intelligent captures;
- basic manual records/reminders;
- core search;
- basic push reminders;
- export/delete rights.

### Cuevaro Plus
Target hypothesis: roughly USD 3.99–6.99/month or discounted annual equivalent, localized by market.
- higher/unlimited practical record limits subject to fair use;
- AI extraction;
- policy intelligence where supported;
- advanced lifecycle automation;
- action packs;
- cross-device sync;
- richer search/history.

### Cuevaro Family
Target hypothesis: roughly USD 6.99–10.99/month or annual equivalent.
- shared household;
- multiple members;
- shared ownership/cues;
- household roles;
- higher usage limits.

### Cuevaro Business / Teams — later
Pricing should be separate because compliance, audit, support, employee certifications, vendor contracts, and team administration carry different value and cost.

Do not lock these numbers before willingness-to-pay tests and unit economics.

## 8. Trial strategy

Prefer a value-triggered trial:
- first several intelligent captures free;
- show the user the lifecycle value created;
- invite upgrade when they need more automation, household sharing, or advanced intelligence.

Avoid asking for payment before Cuevaro has successfully understood at least one real item.

## 9. Acquisition

Early channels:
- App Store / Google Play search around warranty tracker, receipt organizer, return reminder, home inventory, expiry tracker;
- short product demos showing "photo → useful future action";
- homeowner, parent, expat, organization, and personal-finance communities without spam;
- SEO pages around concrete jobs-to-be-done;
- partnerships later with appliance retailers, service providers, property/insurance ecosystems only when incentives align.

Do not start with broad "AI life assistant" marketing. Sell one painful outcome at a time.

## 10. Launch message examples

- "Photograph the receipt. Cuevaro handles what comes next."
- "The receipt, warranty, return window, and next step stay together."
- "Stop remembering admin. Remember your life."
- "Send it once. Know when it matters."

Taglines require separate brand/legal review before final adoption.

## 11. Retention loop

Healthy retention should come from accumulated usefulness:

1. capture something valuable;
2. Cuevaro creates a reliable lifecycle;
3. a useful cue arrives at the right time;
4. Cuevaro helps complete the action;
5. the completed action enriches the item's history;
6. trust increases and more real-world items are added.

Do not manufacture engagement. Daily opens are not a success metric for a quiet admin product.

## 12. Core product KPIs

North-star candidate:
**Monthly households receiving at least one useful lifecycle outcome from previously captured evidence.**

Supporting:
- time to first useful confirmed record;
- capture → confirmed-record conversion;
- percent of records requiring no manual correction;
- useful cue action rate;
- high-consequence false-confidence rate;
- reminder delivery reliability;
- records with source/provenance;
- 30/90-day household retention;
- paid conversion after demonstrated value;
- support contacts per 1,000 captures;
- AI + infrastructure cost per confirmed intelligent capture;
- export/delete success rate.

Vanity metrics such as raw notification count should not drive design.

## 13. Commercial launch gates

Before charging:
- core mobile flow is faster than manual calendar/file setup;
- pricing matches measured unit economics;
- privacy/security disclosures match the implementation;
- data export and deletion work;
- refund/support path exists;
- app-store billing requirements are satisfied;
- brand and trademark risk has been reviewed;
- no claim promises guaranteed warranty/return eligibility Cuevaro cannot prove.

## 14. Expansion sequence

After purchase lifecycle proves retention:
1. home maintenance and service;
2. vehicles and registrations;
3. important personal/family documents and expiries;
4. school/family admin ingestion;
5. insurance evidence and claim packs;
6. subscriptions/contracts only where Cuevaro adds more than a generic reminder;
7. Cuevaro Business for certification/vendor/asset lifecycles.

Expansion must reuse the same lifecycle engine. Do not create disconnected mini-apps.
